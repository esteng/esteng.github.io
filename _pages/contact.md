If you are a student looking to get in touch, please read the following:

**General guidance**: Please see Yonatan Bisk's helpful [guide on emailing faculty](https://talkingtorobots.com/FAQ.html). A note about AI-generated emails: these generally all look the same, don't reflect well on the sender, and I do not read them. If you do email me, please keep your note as brief and information-dense as possible.

**Questions about PhD admissions**: Unfortunately I cannot reply to every email. If you feel drawn to the topics/publications listed on my webpage, please mention me in your graduate application and I will read it. Please do not email to inquire whether you should apply: I cannot determine that without seeing your application. If you apply, I will be able to read all your materials more efficiently.

**Questions about internships**: I'm not taking interns at this time.

**Questions about courses**: Please use the course page/canvas where possible. If you must email me, please include the course number in the subject line.
