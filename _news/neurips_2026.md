Two papers accepted to NeurIPS 2026! [AVSD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20643) improves self-distillation by adaptively balancing consensus across views with teacher-specific privileged signals, and [LongMINT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18565) evaluates how well long-horizon agents' memory holds up under multi-target interference.
